Folkzen

The AI-driven operating system for small businesses.

Folkzen gives growing companies everything they need to run their people operations in one calm platform: onboarding, timesheets, leave, payroll-ready reporting, compliance tracking — plus invoicing generated straight from approved timesheets, Ask Folkzen natural-language reporting, and automatic expiry alerts.

  • Employee onboarding with document checklists and verifications
  • Timesheets with client approval workflows
  • Invoicing from approved timesheets — bill rates, PO burn-down, PDFs

  • Leave management with team calendars
