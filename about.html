About Varant Inc

A technology partner built for the long term.

For over 15 years, we've helped enterprises modernize their technology and ship products their customers love.

Our story

Consultants who build. Builders who consult.

Varant Inc was founded on a simple belief: the best technology advice comes from people who actually build software. That principle has carried us from early consulting engagements to long-term partnerships with Fortune 1000 clients.

Today we operate from our headquarters in Coppell, Texas, with a delivery center in Navi Mumbai, India — giving our clients onshore partnership backed by global engineering depth.

And we practice what we preach: our product lab has shipped InvestWick, a financial advisory platform, and Staffline, HR software for small and medium businesses — proof that our teams don't just advise on products, they deliver them.

We're AI-native in practice, not in slideware. Our engineers ship RAG pipelines over enterprise knowledge bases, autonomous agents with tool use and multi-step planning, and ML forecasting systems running in production — the same stack inside InvestWick's market intelligence engine. Every AI engagement ships with eval harnesses, guardrails, and observability, because a model that can't be measured can't be trusted. From vector search to red-teaming, we do the unglamorous engineering that makes AI actually work.
